Power BI Completed

Warehouse & Logistics Cost Optimization

Analysed ~50,000 e-commerce orders to determine the actual factors in late deliveries and evaluate whether freight cost trade-offs justify keeping the worst-performing carrier.

  • Weather is the strongest driver of on-time delivery (65% in clear conditions vs. 6.5% in snow); quarter and warehouse ID were also tested but ruled-out.
  • Freight-to-value ratio is flat (~65%) across every carrier and the worst-performing carrier isn't cheaper, so there's no cost trade-off justifying its use.
  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • Power Query
  • Data Cleaning

Tableau Completed

Seattle Airbnb Analysis

Analysis on the Seattle Airbnb dataset exploring host performance, pricing trends, and neighborhood dynamics.

  • Superhost status barely moves price (~2% difference) but strongly predicts host response rate (~18-point gap) — consistent with response rate being one of Airbnb's actual criteria for superhost status.
  • Magnolia has the highest average price despite a below-average superhost % and response rate, because of the highest average number of bedrooms of any neighborhood.

Python Completed

LEGO Set Clustering Analysis

Clustered 18,600+ Rebrickable LEGO sets by release year and part count using K-Means, applying a Standard Scaler to normalize the axes and the Elbow method to determine the optimal number of clusters.

  • LEGO's growth comes from expanding across product tiers (small to 5,000+ part mega sets) released together recently, not from sets simply growing bigger over time.
  • 2022 was excluded from the analysis due to incomplete data — extrapolated from a sharp, isolated drop in set count that year relative to the surrounding trend.

  • scikit-learn
  • K-Means
  • pandas
