A Bit More About Me

Data by trade,
curious by nature

I play badminton, volleyball, and soccer — volleyball especially, having represented my school. It taught me what dashboards can't: that consistent effort and good teamwork beat individual brilliance every time.

When I'm not on a court, I'm exploring. I travel solo, chasing hidden natural gems over well-worn tourist trails, and I'm endlessly drawn to historic art and architecture — there's a kind of data story in how things were built to last.

I read voraciously — sci-fi for the "what if," self-help for the "what now" — and I try to carry that same appetite for adventure into my work, not just my downtime.

And yes, I'm a genuine LEGO enthusiast — not just the sets, but the company. How LEGO manages product strategy across price tiers, where data shapes those calls — it's a rabbit hole I love going down. It's also why my first independent Python project wasn't a toy dataset, but a clustering analysis of 18,600+ LEGO sets.
